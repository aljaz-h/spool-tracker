Unlike the topbar's own trigger buttons (inside a sticky header, so they never move once scrolled to the top), this Filters button sits in the ordinary scrolling page - the panel's top/left are only computed once, on open (getBoundingClientRect), so without @scroll.window closing it, scrolling the page leaves the fixed-positioned panel visually stuck at that original screen position instead of following (or disappearing with) the button it's meant to be anchored to. {% endcomment %}
