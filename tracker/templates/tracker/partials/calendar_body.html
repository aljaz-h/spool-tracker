{% comment %} align-items:stretch can't express "sidebar always matches cal-main's height exactly, whichever is naturally taller" - stretch only grows the shorter item up to the taller one's own content height, so a long agenda list just dragged the whole row (and cal-main with it) down to its own height instead of getting capped, while a short one left empty space below a cal-main-sized box instead of shrinking to match. Explicitly copying cal-main's rendered height onto the sidebar's own height (JS, via ResizeObserver so month navigation/content swaps keep it in sync) pins the sidebar to cal-main in both directions - overflow-y-auto then handles a longer agenda list by scrolling inside that fixed height instead of growing past it. No-ops below the lg: breakpoint, where they stack instead of sitting side by side - the sidebar's own max-h-[60vh] (below) is what caps it there instead, since there's no cal-main height to match against once they're stacked. Without either, the agenda (up to AGENDA_LOOKBACK_DAYS of past releases plus everything upcoming) just grows the page forever on mobile. items-stretch below lg: is a different axis from the height-matching discussion above, not a contradiction of it - flex-col's cross axis is width, not height, and items-start (no stretch) there was leaving #cal-main sized to its own intrinsic content width instead of filling the stacked column, which starved calendar_main.html's grid-cols-7 of width it should've had (visible as the day grid running noticeably narrower than the page with empty space to its right on a phone). items-start still governs the lg:flex-row cross axis (height) exactly as described above - only the mobile/stacked axis changed. {% endcomment %}
