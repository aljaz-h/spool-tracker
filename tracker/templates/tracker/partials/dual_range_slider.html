Position math accounts for the 16px thumb (see .dual-range-thumb in app.css) - a plain 0-100% mapping would place the fill bar/labels at the container's true edges, while the native thumb's own center never quite reaches them (browsers keep a range input's thumb fully inside its track, so at value=max the thumb's center sits at 100% - half its width, not literally 100%). calc(fraction * (100% - 16px) + 8px) matches that native positioning instead of drifting from it. x-cloak keeps the fill bar/labels hidden until Alpine has applied that calculation at least once, instead of briefly showing the pre-hydration unstyled (effectively full-width) state. Each thumb uses :value + @input (not x-model) so the handler can clamp against the *other* thumb's current value before writing - otherwise dragging one handle past the other produces an inverted range (from > to) that neither the fill bar nor the TMDB query params behind it were ever designed to handle. {% endcomment %}
